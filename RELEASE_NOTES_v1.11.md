# Project 1989 — The Manager's Desk | v1.11.0 Narrative Beta

**YOUR CLUB. YOUR DECISIONS. YOUR LEGACY.**

## The football world now has a voice

- Real-club careers recognise classic English Football League rivalries (Merseyside, North London, Manchester, Second City, East Midlands, Sheffield, Devon and many others). Rivalry pairs remain meaningful even if promotions/relegations separate clubs.
- PES-style careers use their fictional club names and **explicitly fictional rivalries**, never imply that an invented derby is an actual historical football rivalry. Every fictional club can have a paired local grudge match.
- Rival clubs have deterministic, **fictional** named managers with stable styles, carried across save/reload by the career seed. They are *not* claims about who historically managed the clubs in 1989.
- Club inspection and match preview show opposition manager and derby information. A rivalry preview also appears in the Manager's Desk briefing when the next fixture is a derby.
- Press Cuttings contain multi-paragraph reports about the actual result, named scorers and minutes, shots and possession when available, league position and distance from first place. Manager comments are fictional and explicitly labelled.
- League round-ups, title-race bulletins, relegation pressure, derby build-up and aftermath use actual simulated results/standings. No false goals, standings or fixtures are invented for narrative flavour.
- Project title and slogan refreshed while retaining stadium backdrop, typewriter typography and facing-page ring-bound notebook. Nothing about the match engine, player ratings or 92-club historical data has been changed in this release.

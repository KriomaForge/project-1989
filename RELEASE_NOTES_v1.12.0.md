# Project 1989 — The Manager's Desk v1.12.0

## Season Narrative Review — Football League Gazette special

Every completed season now creates a persistent newspaper edition based on real in-career statistics: final league position, record, promotion or relegation, biggest win, heaviest loss, strongest derby narrative, leading reported goalscorer, late run-in and cup highlights when available.

Read it in Season Review. Previous editions remain selectable as back issues after the next season begins. The full league promotion/relegation and cups listing is preserved under **Official League Records and Cup Winners**. A press story is also added to Press Cuttings.

The editorial text is entirely in-world; all results and goalscorers are taken from simulation data. No real historical match outcomes are invented. Older saved seasons cannot recover match detail absent from their save, but remain otherwise playable. No match engine or player rating changes.

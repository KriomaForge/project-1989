# Project 1989 v1.15.0 — The Club and the Crowd (Beta)

- New Supporters notebook from the Manager’s Office and Ground File, with mood, confidence, gate records and three simple ticket policies.
- Home league gate receipts now respond to estimated demand, prices, recent form and supporter mood. Attendance is bounded by illustrative club-size capacity, not claimed historical ground figures.
- Supporters react to wins, defeats and heavy home losses; exceptional pressure or confidence causes only a *small, capped* morale/board response.
- Terrace newspaper stories use actual match scores; heavy home defeats can trigger boos.
- Existing careers upgrade on load with a neutral supporter profile. Existing results and finances are retained.
- Stadium construction, shops, facilities, actual historical capacity research and venue-specific highlights remain planned milestones.
- Economic parameters are game-balance estimates rather than verified 1989 attendance, ticket or financial history.

## Balance check
Ran 60 full 1989–90 seasons across Liverpool, Villa, Chelsea, West Brom, Wolves, Walsall, Notts County, Exeter, Maidstone and Burnley with value, standard and premium ticket policies (two seeds each). Three finished below starting cash; 57 remained cash-positive. Average change per policy in this sample: value +£791k, standard +£804k, premium +£781k. This confirms continuing cash accumulation in the existing economy, so future upkeep/building and income tuning must be carefully tested; it does not prove premium prices are economically optimal for every club. These samples include season prize distributions and are not historical records.

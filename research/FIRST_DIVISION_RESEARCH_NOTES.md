# 1989–90 First Division: registration verification audit

**Status: Not complete.** This release creates a research register for all 20 First Division clubs and locates corresponding 1989–90 **season-appearance** pages. It additionally stages a player-level Aston Villa list. It does not claim to have verified the full August 1989 player registrations.

## Evidence levels

1. **Registration-level verified:** player named on a dated Football League registration record effective on or before the selected start date, and not known to have departed. **0 of 20 clubs cleared.**
2. **Season-associated:** player appears in a documented 1989–90 club roster; may have joined later (e.g. Dwight Yorke).
3. **Generated:** simulation-only player; never claim historical accuracy.

## Primary research bottleneck

The National Football Museum archive catalogue confirms the Football League's **players’ registration books 1890–1992**, with registration dates and appearances for all clubs. The actual 1989 pages are not published in the catalogue and must be consulted or obtained by permission. Source: https://www.nationalfootballmuseum.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/NFM-Archive-list.pdf

## Published squad evidence

11v11's 1989–90 Aston Villa roster identifies 24 actual squad members (excluding the own-goal entry). Source: https://www.11v11.com/teams/aston-villa/tab/players/season/1990/ . **Season membership does not prove opening-day registration.**

## Next verification steps

- Obtain registration-ledger evidence for each First Division club (dates and contemporaneous transfers), subject to museum access terms.
- Supplement with club season programmes, dated opening fixtures, and independent player biographies.
- Record full birth dates only when sourced, calculate age on 1 August 1989, and leave missing birth dates explicitly uncertain.
- After verification, import historical squads via a separate evidence-gated database, never overwrite active careers, and independently assign approximate game ability and potential.

**Avoid:** treating all season-long appearances as a starting squad; copying third-party compilations without checking reuse terms; or filling gaps and labelling the result as authentic.

What is simulated?

Match results, individual ability ratings, transfers, club finances, attendance, supporter mood, ground capacity and improvements, managerial quotes, appointments, and newspaper opinions are original gameplay simulations. Opposition managers are game-created characters, not authenticated 1989 appointments.

Reference material

  • Football Club History Database — 1989–90 English Football League tables (historical league context).
  • Londonist — Football grounds in the 1980s (atmosphere, stadium-era references).
  • 11v11 — football historical player reference (private research background only; no unrestricted republication permission).
  • 11v11 terms and conditions (copyright and database-right restrictions).
  • Period football match programmes and 1980s ground photographs provided as design inspiration; original game artwork and text are not reproductions of those works.

Historical datasets & permission

The historical 92-club player data used in the local research build includes source-derived records whose redistribution status is unresolved. The public-testing package is provided without that bundled player research and with historical-player modes disabled until permission or an independently cleared alternative is established. Historical clubs with generated squads and the alternative PES-style world remain playable.

Playtesting

Use Save / Load to export your career before updating. Report the club, mode, week, expected behaviour, actual behaviour and browser. Financial and match results are gameplay estimates rather than archival records.

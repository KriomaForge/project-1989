## Rights status
11v11 places copyright/database restrictions on reproduction and redistribution. A citation does not confer redistribution rights. The full historical-data bundle is a PRIVATE LOCAL RESEARCH build, not approved for public GitHub or third-party distribution. The Github-safe tester package excludes sourced player datasets and disables historical-player modes. Seek permission before publishing a historical-data version.

## Simulation / attribution
Names and identities of game managers, outcomes, tactical quotations, attendance and capacity figures are simulated; real club names and period-inspired settings serve an unofficial historical fiction game.

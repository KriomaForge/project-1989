# Project 1989 v1.9.2 — Three football worlds (local research preview)

Choose one of three new-game modes:

1. **Real clubs + generated squads**: the established game.
2. **Real clubs + historical roster research PREVIEW**: only Aston Villa and Exeter City have selected source-verified 1989–90 season names and positions. All other clubs use fictional squads and even those two clubs include generated fillers. **This is not a full authentic 92-club data pack.** Source names reflect season participation, not necessarily opening-day registration. Player ages, skill, potential, wage and transfer values are all simulations.
3. **Fictional English football world**: 92 unique generated fictional club names, four divisions and generated squads, with the same game engine. Fictional club names are original, not imported PES/Konami assets.

Existing careers continue in their original world (save version 3). New mode selection applies only to new careers.

Sources for historical preview: [11v11 Aston Villa 1989–90](https://www.11v11.com/teams/aston-villa/tab/players/season/1990/); [11v11 Exeter City 1989–90](https://www.11v11.com/teams/exeter-city/tab/players/season/1990/). Historical names and positions are facts; do not republish source page text or images. Source conditions should be checked before distributing a full bulk dataset.

Further research needed: verify season-opening squads and dates for the remaining 90 clubs; validate dates of birth and positions independently, then produce a complete authentic pack with per-row source citations.

# Project 1989 v1.9.0 Beta 1 — Club Colours & Academy Intake

## Included
- Simplified home shirt palettes for all 92 starting clubs. Half-and-half colour markers appear beside club names in league tables, fixtures, cup results, match scoreboards and the notebook.
- Retro replay pixel footballers wear the **home** club palette. The **away** side always wears white (deliberate visual convention rather than historically accurate away kits).
- Each club generates **five** youngsters aged 16–18 at the beginning of a career and at season rollover: **two automatically join the club squad** and **three are released** into the free-transfer market. This creates 184 squad promotions and 276 available youth free agents per intake.
- Starting ability uses academy/division context; exceptional potential can emerge at every level.
- Save schema remains v3. Existing saved squads are not retroactively changed; next season the new intake rule takes effect.

## Historical scope
This is the **v1.9 foundation build**, not a historical squad-data release. Existing player names and attributes remain fictional. Club colours are approximations based on period identity, not reproductions of exact 1989–90 shirts. Third-party kit artwork is not bundled. Historical named squad research, source validation, permission review and a historical/fictional start switch remain future work.

## Tests
Tests cover 92 mapped clubs, academy/free-agent counts, new-season transitions, and home/away replay colour mapping. Visual browser testing remains necessary before public deployment.

# Project 1989 — v1.9.6 First Division Research & Club Inspection

- Click a club name in **League Tables** to inspect its current in-game squad (all four divisions; all game worlds). Read-only; no transfers or player edits.
- Displays player names, positions, age, ability, fitness, form and goals. History mode is explicitly labelled *preview*, with simulated ratings and estimated/uncertain ages.
- Research folder includes a First Division 20-club audit with individually located season-statistics source pages, an Aston Villa 1989–90 **season** roster evidence file, and source/provenance guidance.
- **No full opening-day squads have been verified** from player registration books. The research archive is not a completed historical squad pack.
- Existing save files and fixture systems are unchanged.

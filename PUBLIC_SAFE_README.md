# Public testing edition — generated-player worlds only

This bundle contains the game, 92 historical club identities and two playable generated-player worlds. Its 1989–90 research player database, private research pilot and historical squad modes have been excluded intentionally. Do not upload the private research build in its place without permission from relevant data rights holders.

This restriction also prevents importing historical-data career JSON saves into this edition. Continue historical careers in the separate private build. See SOURCES_AND_RIGHTS.md.

## Simulation calibration

48 completed 1989–90 careers across Liverpool, Aston Villa, West Brom, Walsall, Exeter and Maidstone (12 each baseline/terrace/facilities/stand). Cash changes include the upfront construction investment, while clubs began each comparison with identical initial cash for scenario testing. The average net year-end changes were £928,640 with no works, £876,527 with a terrace, £968,235 with shops/refreshments and £611,723 with a stand. These figures are game-economy outputs, not historic finances or a guarantee of returns.
